Checked October 24, 2026: 48 Kimi metrics and 29 existing peer report records. All 77 report values matched their original provider sources. This verifies transcription, not independent reproducibility.

The publisher comparison reproduces one source table, in source column order. All six Kimi-table columns were captured: 252 numeric cells and 36 missing cells. Five numeric cells are flagged outside the plot (two text-only subsets, one older DeepSeek model result and two Fable/Mythos identity conflicts). These competitor columns are Moonshot’s published comparisons, not independently validated competitor results. Solid bars use audited benchmark-maintainer evaluations. Dashed reports are unranked. Twelve metric charts have captured official rankings; only three currently contain Kimi K3.1 Pro. The other 36 have no eligible official ranking in this audit; reasons are listed individually below.

No score is substituted across versions, metrics, subsets, tools or unclear provenance. Missing records are not zeros. Official boards can contain different efforts and harnesses; read run settings before drawing capability conclusions.
