# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve the Benchmark Radar index.

## Corrections

Open an issue with the Benchmark name, its arXiv or official project URL, the field that is incorrect, and a primary-source link supporting the correction.

## New Benchmark suggestions

A suggested entry should introduce, extend, or aggregate an evaluation Benchmark. Please include:

- the canonical Benchmark name;
- the paper or official report;
- the first public release date;
- code, dataset, or project links when available;
- a source sentence showing what the Benchmark evaluates.

Ordinary papers that only compare models on existing Benchmarks are not new Benchmark entries.

## Review principles

- Primary sources take precedence over third-party summaries.
- Missing information remains unknown rather than inferred.
- Popularity does not imply quality or readiness.
- Different releases or evaluation protocols should not be merged when their results are not comparable.
